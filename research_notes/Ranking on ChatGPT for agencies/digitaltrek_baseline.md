# Digital Trek (digitaltrek.ca): Public Web Footprint and Canadian AI-Recommendation Landscape, October 2026

## Who is Digital Trek (digitaltrek.ca), what does it offer, and is the site readable by AI crawlers?

### Method note (read first)
- None of the pages could be fetched directly. The sandbox egress proxy refused connections to digitaltrek.ca (HTTP 403 on CONNECT), and WebFetch returned DNS errors (`ENOTFOUND`) for every domain tried: digitaltrek.ca, www.digitaltrek.ca, clutch.co, themanifest.com, reviews.birdeye.com and leadstal.com. Every finding below therefore comes from WebSearch result snippets and the search engine's summaries of indexed pages. Treat each one as second-hand, and confirm anything load-bearing (prices, review counts, dates) on the live page before it goes into client-facing material.
- That limit also means robots.txt, llms.txt, the sitemap and the raw JSON-LD could not be inspected (see Gaps).

### Takeaway
Digital Trek is a small Vancouver, BC digital marketing agency founded and run by Mario Micka. It positions itself for home-service trades (roofers, plumbers, electricians) and healthcare clinics, and sells local SEO, Google Ads, web design, CRO and a fractional CMO service. Its main entity-recognition weaknesses are inconsistent addresses (Vancouver, Surrey and New Westminster), a conflicting founding year (2021 vs 2011) and review counts that differ between sources.

### Inferences
- Three addresses in three municipalities (Vancouver, Surrey, New Westminster) across the site, LinkedIn, Digital Main Street, Leadstal and ZoomInfo are a classic NAP-consistency problem. That weakens local-pack and entity signals that Bing, Google and AI assistants reconcile. The same applies to the 2011/2021 founding-year conflict and the varying review counts (50, 52 and 57).
- "Trek Marketing" (Vancouver, larger, older, with BBB, DesignRush, G2 and listicle coverage) is the most serious confusion risk: an assistant asked about a "Vancouver Trek marketing agency" may surface Trek Marketing instead. Trek Marketing also targets dental clinics, which overlaps Digital Trek's healthcare niche.
- Search engines are clearly indexing many digitaltrek.ca pages, so the site is not blocking general search crawlers. Whether it treats GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot or ChatGPT-User differently is unknown.
- The healthcare vertical has no proof points (case studies) published, while trades has two. This is an asymmetry in what an AI assistant could cite.
- The mix of older generic AI/B2B posts with three different tagline-style title formats dilutes topical focus. The duplicated "-2" slug suggests leftover WordPress duplicates.

## Canadian and French-language (Quebec) considerations

### Takeaway
French-language and Quebec considerations matter little for Digital Trek, whose market is Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley. Two Canadian factors matter more. First, "Vancouver" queries are contaminated by Vancouver, Washington results. Second, the national "best agency" lists are Toronto-heavy and include Quebec-specific firms.

### Cited Findings
- **Vancouver, WA contamination:**
  - Clutch's non-/ca/ Vancouver list mixes in Vancouver, WA and Beaverton, OR agencies. — [Clutch Vancouver](https://clutch.co/agencies/digital-marketing/vancouver)
  - Expertise.com publishes a "Vancouver, WA" digital marketing agencies list. — [Expertise Vancouver WA](https://www.expertise.com/business/digital-marketing-agencies/washington/vancouver)
  - Some chiropractic-marketing results for "Vancouver" are Vancouver, WA agencies. — [Genius Marketing](https://geniusmarketingco.com/locations/vancouver-wa/chiropractic-marketing/)
  - A "Trek Global" agency is listed in Vancouver, WA. — [ppc4.com](https://ppc4.com/digital-marketing/washington/vancouver/trek-global)
- **Quebec in national lists:** Social Media 55 (Montreal) and Digitad are the Quebec options that recur, and a Quebec agency publishes a "20 Best Digital Marketing Agencies in Canada" list. — [Agence One Way](https://www.agenceoneway.ca/blog/top-marketing-agencies-canada-2025); [GoodFirms Canada](https://www.goodfirms.co/directory/country/top-digital-marketing-companies/ca)
- **Quebec directory name-alike:** hellodarwin, a Quebec-oriented agency directory, lists an agency named "trek it". — [hellodarwin](https://hellodarwin.com/agencies/trek-it)

### Inferences
- Writing "Vancouver, BC" explicitly in titles, schema (addressRegion "BC", addressCountry "CA") and directory profiles helps disambiguate from Vancouver, WA. This matters even more because a "Trek"-named agency is listed in Vancouver, WA.
- National "best in Canada" lists skew toward Toronto, Ottawa and Montreal firms. A Vancouver trades and healthcare specialist is more likely to win city-level and niche-level queries than national ones.
- French-language content is unlikely to be needed unless Digital Trek targets Quebec clients. Nothing found suggests it does.

### Gaps
- Whether digitaltrek.ca has any French pages or hreflang: not verified.
- Whether ChatGPT's answers for "Vancouver" queries actually pull Vancouver, WA agencies was not tested.
