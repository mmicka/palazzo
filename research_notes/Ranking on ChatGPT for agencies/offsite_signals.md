# Off-site signals that drive ChatGPT and other AI assistants to mention and recommend service businesses (digital, marketing, web design, SEO agencies), as of October 2026

> Method note for the report writer: research was done on 27 Oct 2026. Every WebFetch attempt failed in this environment (DNS and proxy errors for ahrefs.com, semrush.com, searchengineland.com, onely.com and others), so the figures below come from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages and from secondary coverage, not from full reads of the primary pages. The numbers are internally consistent across several summaries, but re-check exact figures against the linked primary pages before quoting them. Almost every source is a vendor that sells AI-visibility tracking, PR, syndication, reviews or agency services, so each has a conflict of interest. Nearly all of the evidence is correlational.

## Overall: which off-site factors matter most for "best agency" queries? (evidence-ranked)

### Takeaway
The best-supported off-site levers for agencies are, in order: (1) being listed, and listed high, in third-party "best [service] agencies in [city]" roundups; (2) a broad, consistent volume of brand mentions across the web, including YouTube and earned media; (3) a well-reviewed Clutch profile, since Clutch is the agency-specific domain ChatGPT cites most. LinkedIn, Reddit and review platforms are secondary and volatile. Wikipedia/Wikidata, other directories, paid awards and backlinks have weak or no direct evidence for small agencies. One important counter-finding: a small October 2026 test found ChatGPT, unlike other engines, cites agencies' own websites for more than half of its sources on "best agencies for X" queries. Off-site signals decide whether an agency is in the candidate set, but on-site clarity still matters.

### Cited Findings
**Tier 1: strong, multi-source evidence**
1. **Third-party "best X" listicles/roundups.** Ahrefs analysed 26,283 ChatGPT-cited source URLs from 750 top-of-funnel prompts covering software, products and agencies (e.g., "best web design agencies in London"). "Best X" lists made up 43.8% of cited pages, and a brand's position on third-party lists correlated with whether it was featured — [Ahrefs, best-lists research](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/). Peec AI (~200k responses, 8 engines, 5.7M data points) found that ranking #1 on a frequently cited third-party listicle moved a brand 1.80 answer-positions earlier in US Finance, 1.17 in B2B SaaS and 0.82 in Emerging MarTech, with the effect saturating after a few repeated top placements — [Peec AI, Listicle Rank Effect](https://peec.ai/blog/the-listicle-rank-effect-what-nearly-200-000-ai-responses-across-8-ai-engines-reveal-about-brand-visibility).
2. **Unlinked brand mentions and YouTube mentions.** In Ahrefs' 75,000-brand study, YouTube mentions had the highest correlation with AI visibility (~0.737 Spearman) across ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews. Branded web mentions came in at ~0.656–0.709. ChatGPT showed the weakest link to classic authority metrics (branded search volume 0.352, Domain Rating 0.266) — [The Next Web on Ahrefs](https://thenextweb.com/news/ahrefs-youtube-mentions-ai-visibility-brand-search); [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations).
3. **Clutch.** Surfer's September 2026 ChatGPT data (about 5.73M citations, 1–28 Sept 2026) ranks clutch.co as the #2 most-cited domain in ChatGPT with 25,864 citations, behind only reddit.com (42,623) — [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/blog/most-cited-domains-chatgpt/).
4. **Earned media/digital PR.** Muck Rack's May 2026 study of 25M+ links cited by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini classified 84% as earned media (journalism 27%) and paid content as 0.3% — [Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com/blog/what-is-ai-reading-may-2026).

**Tier 2: moderate or volatile evidence**
5. **LinkedIn**, especially individual people's profiles and posts: it rose from about #11 to #5 in ChatGPT citations between Nov 2025 and Feb 2026 — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/linkedin-is-the-most-cited-domain-for-professional-queries-in-ai-search).
6. **Reddit**: it has a huge overall citation share but very volatile visibility, and it was only 1% of citations in Onely's agency-query test — [Onely](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/).
7. **Review platforms (Trustpilot, Google reviews, Yelp for local, G2 for software).** Trustpilot is the most-cited review site, more than 2x the next one, across 804k responses — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-of-800k-ai-responses-how-reviews-shape-brand-presence-in-ai-search).

**Tier 3: weak, anecdotal or unproven**
8. Secondary agency directories (DesignRush, GoodFirms, Sortlist, UpCity, Agency Spotter). The only citation count comes from a 50-answer audit — [SCALZ.AI](https://scalz.ai/blog/agency-directories-ai-citations/).
9. Wikidata, Crunchbase and knowledge-graph work: there are no measured before/after results — [LLMrefs](https://llmrefs.com/blog/chatgpt-entities); [ARGEO](https://www.argeo.ai/en/blog/knowledge-graph-wikidata-optimization).
10. Paid awards: "0 studies" measure their effect on AI visibility — [Stanislav Peev](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/owned-review-sites-ai-visibility/).
11. Self-promotional listicles: they give a small lift and carry growing risk (see the self-promotion section below).
12. Backlinks and DR: backlinks correlated at 0.218 in the original AI Overviews study — [Ahrefs, May 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/branded-web-mentions-visibility-ai-search/).

**Counterweight (on-site):** Across 31 "best agencies for X" questions (October 2026 answers), ChatGPT cited the named agencies' own sites for 119 of 210 cited pages (57%), with lists at 22% and Reddit at 1%. Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Mode drew 59–77% of their citations from lists — [Onely](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/). Onely is itself an SEO agency and the sample is small.

### Inferences
- The two studies can be reconciled: listicles and third-party mentions get an agency into the candidate set ChatGPT considers, and ChatGPT then often visits and cites the shortlisted agencies' own sites to confirm services and location. So off-site work is needed to be shortlisted, and the agency's own site supports the final mention.
- For a small agency, the realistic order of operations is: Clutch profile plus reviews → get onto local or niche "best agencies in [city]/[niche]" lists that are already cited → steady earned mentions (guest posts, podcasts, YouTube, LinkedIn by founders) → Reddit and community participation where genuine.
- Because ChatGPT's correlation with DR and branded search is the weakest of the three platforms Ahrefs measured, smaller agencies may have a better shot in ChatGPT than in Google AI Mode, which Ahrefs called a "consensus engine" favouring well-known brands.

### Gaps
- No independent, large-sample study isolates agency or service-provider queries specifically. Ahrefs' 750-prompt set includes agencies but does not report agency-only figures in the summaries available.
- No study measures causal lift from any single off-site tactic for an agency.

## Which domains does ChatGPT cite most, overall and for B2B service/agency queries?

### Takeaway
Overall, Reddit and Wikipedia lead ChatGPT citations, with Forbes, YouTube and LinkedIn behind them. Which of Reddit and Wikipedia comes first depends on the tracker. For agency and B2B-service queries, Clutch is the standout: Surfer's September 2026 data puts it at #2 in ChatGPT overall, a sign of how heavily agency/vendor queries draw on it. The exact percentages vary sharply by tracker, prompt set and month.

### Cited Findings
- **Ahrefs (September 2026):** Reddit 16.8%, Wikipedia 7.0%, Consumer Reports 3.7%, Forbes 3.1%, YouTube 2.5%, Healthline 2.5%. July 2026: Reddit 16.7%, Wikipedia 8.9%, Forbes 3rd, with long-tail sites like EngineerFix and LegalClarity also in the top 10 — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/).
- **Surfer (1–28 Sept 2026, about 5.73M ChatGPT citations from Surfer users' tracked prompts):** reddit.com #1 (42,623 citations), clutch.co #2 (25,864; 5.35% of top-50 citations, 0.45% of all citations) — [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/blog/most-cited-domains-chatgpt/). Clutch is also #9 in Google AI Mode (12,528) — [Surfer AI Mode](https://surferseo.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-mode/) — and #18 in AI Overviews (9,590) — [Surfer AIO](https://surferseo.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/).
- **Similarweb (US, Jan–Feb 2026, ~600,000 citation events), via a 5W PR synthesis:** Wikipedia 13.15%, Reddit 11.97%; no other domain exceeds 3%; WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg and FT are not in the top 20 — [5W/MarTech Edge release](https://martechedge.com/press-release/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds----wsj-nyt-and-bloomberg-do-not-appear-in-the-top-20).
- **Everything-PR multi-engine index (2026):** Reddit, Wikipedia, YouTube, LinkedIn and Forbes are most cited across engines. For ChatGPT specifically, Wikipedia, Forbes and Amazon lead — [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/chatgpt-citation-source-index-2026).
- **LinkedIn:** about #11 in ChatGPT citations in Nov 2025, about #5 by Feb 2026 (more than 2x citation frequency), and #1 for "professional" queries across AI search — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/linkedin-is-the-most-cited-domain-for-professional-queries-in-ai-search). Meltwater (9.5M citations, all engines) ranks LinkedIn #2 for B2B queries behind YouTube, with its share up 26% over a 4-week window — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/report-looks-at-how-linkedin-is-dominating-b2b-queries-in-ai-chatbots/820946/); [Financial Narrative](https://financialnarrative.org/news/11554881).
- **Review sites:** Trustpilot is the most-cited review website across ChatGPT, AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity (804,491 responses, March 2026), more than 2x the next review site. ChatGPT had the highest Trustpilot citation rate of the four, at 57.9% after controlling for brand size — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-of-800k-ai-responses-how-reviews-shape-brand-presence-in-ai-search). Trustpilot's own release says review/trust sites are the #2 source type at 14% of AI citations — [Trustpilot via PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20813/release/20260512EN56379). This is vendor-published.
- **B2B software (not agencies):** ChatGPT cites a recommended tool's own site 11.6% of the time, and G2, Capterra and TrustRadius combined are 0.9% of citations (40 categories) — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/b2b-saas-ai-citation-study/). SourceRank AI found 8 major B2B review directories (incl. G2, Clutch, Capterra) cited in only 6 of 500 answers (1.2%) — [SourceRank AI](https://sourcerankai.com/blog/do-g2-and-clutch-reviews-help-ai-citation/). A G2-commissioned analysis put review platforms at 7.4–13.2% of ChatGPT citations — [Omnibound summary](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/do-review-sites-g2-capterra-drive-ai-citations-b2b). Hall (2025, outdated) put G2 at 8.25% of ChatGPT citations for B2B software queries — [Hall](https://usehall.com/guides/review-platform-ai-citation-analysis).
- **Retrieval overlap (Aug 2025, flagged older):** only about 8% of ChatGPT-cited URLs ranked in Google's or Bing's top 10 for the same prompt, versus nearly 1 in 3 for Perplexity (15,000 prompts) — [Ahrefs, AI search overlap](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/).

### Inferences
- Clutch's #2 rank in Surfer's data probably reflects Surfer's user base, which is marketers and agencies tracking agency/vendor prompts, more than ChatGPT's traffic overall. It is still strong evidence that ChatGPT leans on Clutch heavily when the prompt is about agencies.
- ChatGPT cites pages that rank outside the classic top 10 (the 8% overlap), because its fan-out sub-queries pull in long-tail pages. A niche "best web design agencies in Halifax" list on a low-authority site can therefore be cited.
- The trackers diverge because they use different prompt sets (consumer vs B2B), geographies and collection methods (API vs UI scraping). No single "top domains" list should be treated as universal.

### Gaps
- I found no published domain ranking restricted to agency/service-provider prompts at large sample size.
- I could not confirm an OpenAI-domain share figure reported in some summaries of the Similarweb data, so it is omitted.
- The Clutch citation-lift claims ("8.6X lift for verified profiles", attributed to a Clutch May 2026 dashboard) are unverified — [ivristech](https://ivristech.com/trustpilot-chatgpt-citations-b2b/). A "Clutch is ~2.4% of AI citations" claim cites unnamed analyses — [tofubofu](https://tofubofu.com/engines/clutch).

## How important are "best X agencies" listicles, and do self-promotional listicles work? What are the risks?

### Takeaway
Third-party listicles are the single most consistently evidenced off-site driver of "best X" recommendations. Self-promotional listicles, where an agency ranks itself #1 on its own blog, give only a small, conditional lift. The lift is largest for unknown brands on narrow queries. ChatGPT already cites them less than other engines do, and Google evidence from 2026 shows growing downside: AI Overviews cite them less and omit the author brand, and some sites lost organic visibility.

### Cited Findings
- **Share of citations.** "Best X" lists were 43.8% of 26,283 ChatGPT-cited URLs in Ahrefs' 750-prompt study (software, products, agencies) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/). Peec found listicles to be just under one-fifth of all cited URLs across >1M citations (Feb 2026) — [Peec docs](https://docs.peec.ai/research/why-listicles-get-cited). One secondary write-up reports listicles at about 40.9% for commercial queries in a 75,000-answer/1M-citation dataset — [MEAN CEO](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=8915). That figure is unverified.
- **Low authority is no barrier.** Per secondary coverage of the Ahrefs study, 35% of cited best lists were on low-authority domains — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/). In a practitioner test on a real agency, ChatGPT's commercial "best agencies" answers were built entirely from third-party listicles (some on near-zero-DR domains), and the tested agency appeared in none of them — [Todd O'Rourke](https://toddmorourke.com/learn/how-chatgpt-picks-sources/).
- **Broad vs narrow queries.** One developer's log found 11 of 15 sources for a broad enterprise vendor query were third-party "best X" articles. Vendor sites were cited mainly on narrow questions — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/josephtannous/i-logged-every-source-chatgpt-used-to-recommend-a-b2b-vendor-heres-what-it-actually-reads-2d38).
- **Ahrefs self-promotion experiment (34 own pages, 9,886 answers across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot, 7 Feb–31 May 2026):**
  - For a new brand (Ahrefs Evolve conference): it broke into 72 previously empty answer slots, and 82% of those new mentions came in answers citing Ahrefs' own pages.
  - For an established brand (Brand Radar): only 6% of new mentions came from answers citing its own pages.
  - 43% of answers citing an Evolve page never named Evolve.
  - Query fit mattered: the narrow "best SEO conferences 2026" got the brand into 66.4% of answers, the broad "best marketing conferences 2026" into 15.8%.
  - Source: [Ahrefs experiment](https://ahrefs.com/blog/self-promotional-content-ai-seo-experiment/).
- **Peec (232,000 citations, 13,000 listicles, Dec 2025–Feb 2026, 6 platforms):** about 11% of listicle citations come from self-promotional lists. ChatGPT has the lowest self-promo rate by far, at about 4%, versus about 10–11% for Google AI Mode and Perplexity. There was no evidence of algorithmic correction over the 12 weeks — [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/self-promotional-listicles-analysis-from-232k-citations).
- **Scrunch (about 10,000 cited URLs, 17 May–30 June 2026, 7 surfaces, control set of 1,033 neutral third-party comparisons):** when a brand's own self-ranking list is cited, its recommendation rate rises from about 4% to about 7%, and its chance of being named roughly doubles — [PPC Land on Scrunch](https://ppc.land/self-ranked-brands-gain-ai-recommendations-from-4-to-7-scrunch-finds/).
- **Lily Ray (Google AI Overviews, 100 B2B "best software" queries, 80 triggering AIO):** self-promotional listicles were cited 323 times, and in 224 of those (69%) Google cited the page but did not recommend its author brand — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-self-serving-listicles-recommend-competitors-480573). Her September 2026 update reports AIO citing 38% fewer of these pages than in June, with the author omitted 83% of the time — [Bushletter](https://www.bushletter.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-38-fewer-vendor-listicles-data-shows/).
- **Trend in AIO:** the self-promotional share of "best X" lists cited by AIO fell from 44% to 24% between July and October 2026 (11,025 US commercial queries). Own lists are still associated with being named more often: 45.4% vs 16.3% when the vendor's own list was cited vs not — [Stanislav Peev](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/owned-review-sites-ai-visibility/).
- **Google organic risk:** SaaS/B2B sites with many self-ranking listicles lost 30–50% of organic visibility within weeks in January 2026, concentrated in blog/guide subfolders. Most also had confounding factors: rapid content scaling, suspected AI content at volume, date-refresh title tactics and programmatic patterns — [ALM Corp summary of Lily Ray](https://almcorp.com/blog/self-promotional-listicles-google-rankings-2026/). Google has not named self-promotional listicles in any policy. Secondary sources note that the scaled-content-abuse policy and Google's spam policies on manipulating generative AI responses could apply — [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/self-promotional-listicles-google-rankings-2026/) (verify against Google's spam policy page).
- **Legal risk:** owned "review" sites, self-ranked lists or awards that hide ownership or carry fake reviews are illegal in the US, EU and UK — [Stanislav Peev](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/owned-review-sites-ai-visibility/).
- **Canadian self-promotion is common:** for example, Infinity Digital's blog ranks itself #1 among "ChatGPT marketing companies in Canada" — [Infinity Digital](https://infinitydigital.ca/blog/10-best-chatgpt-marketing-companies-in-canada/).

### Inferences
- For an unknown local agency, one honest, well-structured "best [service] agencies in [city]" page that includes the agency (with real criteria and real competitors) can help for narrow local queries. That matches the Ahrefs Evolve pattern of new brand plus narrow query. Scaled networks of self-ranking lists are high-risk and the benefit shrinks over time.
- The "cited but not recommended" effect (43% in Ahrefs, 69–83% in AIO) means a self-promo list can end up serving competitors.
- The strongest return is from third-party placement and rank. Peec, Scrunch's control and Ahrefs all point the same way.

### Gaps
- No agency-specific self-promotional listicle experiment with ChatGPT-only results was found.
- The Scrunch figures rest on one secondary summary (PPC Land). I could not open the original.

## Role of agency directories and review platforms (Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms, Sortlist, UpCity, Agency Spotter, G2, GBP, Trustpilot, Yelp, Bing Places)

### Takeaway
For agencies, Clutch is the directory with by far the strongest ChatGPT citation evidence. DesignRush and GoodFirms appear but are much less evidenced. There is essentially no data for Sortlist, UpCity or Agency Spotter. Trustpilot dominates review-site citations generally. Google reviews and Yelp drive local "near me" style answers. G2 matters for software, not agencies. Bing Places has no measured evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Clutch:**
  - #2 most-cited domain in ChatGPT (Sept 2026, 25,864 citations) — [Surfer SEO](https://surferseo.com/blog/most-cited-domains-chatgpt/).
  - In a July 2026 audit of 50 AI answers to agency queries (10 queries, Florida markets), Clutch led with 14 appearances, then DesignRush 7, GoodFirms 6 and Semrush Agency Partners 6 — [SCALZ.AI](https://scalz.ai/blog/agency-directories-ai-citations/). This is a small sample and SCALZ is itself an agency.
- **DesignRush:** some placements are paid — [Lemon.io comparison](https://lemon.io/blog/clutch-alternatives/). DesignRush hosts Canada-specific agency category pages — [DesignRush Canada](https://www.designrush.com/agency/digital-agencies/ca).
- **GoodFirms:** it claims to be cited by ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity. That is the platform's own marketing claim — [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/).
- **Sortlist:** described as an AI-plus-human agency-matching platform. No citation data was found — [Lemon.io](https://lemon.io/blog/clutch-alternatives/).
- **Trustpilot:** most-cited review site across 4 engines, more than 2x the next. ChatGPT had the highest Trustpilot citation rate, at 57.9% after controlling for brand size and organic strength. Google AI Mode pulled from Yelp and G2 more than the others — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/study-of-800k-ai-responses-how-reviews-shape-brand-presence-in-ai-search).
- **G2/Capterra (software):** they made up 0.9% of ChatGPT citations in B2B SaaS categories — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/b2b-saas-ai-citation-study/). G2's commissioned data says 7.4–13.2% — [Omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/do-review-sites-g2-capterra-drive-ai-citations-b2b).
- **Local (GBP, Yelp, BBB):** the results below come from aggregated local-AI statistics pages, and individual attributions could not be verified because page fetches failed.
  - Yelp appeared in 80% of ChatGPT local-business answers (BrightLocal, Aug 2026). Steady Demand's 2,880-prompt test found Yelp in about 96% of runs' business-card grounding and Foursquare in 0%.
  - Businesses ChatGPT and Google AI named had a median of 444 Google reviews, versus 192 for those skipped.
  - BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Birdeye and Expertise appeared among cited local sources.
  - BrightLocal's Feb 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults found 45% had used ChatGPT or other gen-AI tools for local recommendations in the past 12 months.
  - Sources: [BCC Interactive local AI stats](https://www.bccinteractive.com/blog/local-ai-search-statistics/); [SEO Sandwitch](https://seosandwitch.com/chatgpt-local-business-statistics/); [LinkTech 20-search study](https://linktechsolutions.io/insights/how-chatgpt-picks-local-businesses); [Elev8 Operations](https://www.elev8operations.com/guides/ai-search-statistics-for-local-businesses-2026).
- **Conflicting local evidence:** one BrightLocal-based figure says about 93% of AI-cited local domains were business websites, while two other studies found editorial or directory sources dominated (e.g., 48.9% city/national editorial) — [DEV Community local study](https://dev.to/scriptmasterlabs01/do-top-ranked-local-businesses-get-recommended-by-chatgpt-70-never-named-and-the-oct-26-1j1h); [LinkTech](https://linktechsolutions.io/insights/how-chatgpt-picks-local-businesses).

### Inferences
- Priority for an agency: Clutch first. Collect volume and recency of verified reviews and use accurate service and location categories, because Clutch category and city pages are themselves the "best agencies in X" lists ChatGPT reads. Then Google Business Profile reviews, which matter for city-qualified prompts. Then one or two of DesignRush and GoodFirms. Trustpilot matters more for consumer-facing businesses than for B2B agencies.
- Paid directory placements, such as some DesignRush slots, are a form of paid listicle inclusion. Their AI value is untested, and they could be discounted if they are disclosed as sponsored.

### Gaps
- No data on Sortlist, UpCity, Agency Spotter or Bing Places citation frequency in ChatGPT.
- No study isolates whether Clutch review count, rating or ranking position predicts ChatGPT mention for a given agency.
- The ChatGPT/Bing relationship to Bing Places is undocumented in the sources found.

## Role of Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, YouTube and podcasts; Reddit's weight and the OpenAI–Reddit deal

### Takeaway
Reddit is one of ChatGPT's two most-cited domains overall, and OpenAI has licensed Reddit data since May 2024. But Reddit's citation share in ChatGPT Search has collapsed twice (September 2025 and August 2026), and it was only 1% of citations for agency queries in one October 2026 test. YouTube mentions are the strongest correlated signal in Ahrefs' data, and LinkedIn is the fastest-rising B2B source, driven by individual profiles. I found no 2025–2026 data on Quora or podcasts specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenAI–Reddit deal:** announced May 2024. It gives OpenAI real-time access to Reddit content via the Data API. A reported value of about $70M/year has never been officially confirmed — [Rankstar](https://rankstar.io/openai-reddit-licensing-deal/). Reddit's data-licensing revenue grew 24% to $43M, with OpenAI and Google its two biggest buyers (Aug 2026) — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/17/reddits-data-revenue-openai-google-buyers/). Reddit's CEO calls both partnerships "very meaningful" — [CJR](https://www.cjr.org/analysis/reddit-winning-ai-licensing-deals-openai-google-gemini-answers-rsl.php). In July 2026, Reddit stock fell on a report that it may not renew its Google AI deal — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/reddit-stock-google-ai-content-deal.html). A report says Reddit is cutting free API/RSS access while Google and OpenAI keep paying, but the dates in that piece are inconsistent — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/reddit-is-cutting-off-free-api-and-rss-access-while-keeping-google-and-openai-paying/).
- **September 2025 drop:** Semrush tracked 230,000+ prompts over 13 weeks. Reddit fell from about 60% of ChatGPT prompt responses in early August to about 10% by mid-September 2025, with Wikipedia also dropping, and there was no systemic drop across other LLMs — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/). Another tracker measured 29.2% → 5.3% and linked it to Google killing the num=100 parameter — [On-Page.ai](https://blog.on-page.ai/?p=8119). Wikipedia visibility rose as Reddit dropped, per Profound data cited by G2 — [G2 Learn](https://learn.g2.com/reddit-chatgpt-citations).
- **August 2026 drop:** Promptwatch data shows reddit.com fell from 3.83% of ChatGPT Search citations (18 July–7 Aug 2026) to 0.52% (14–17 Aug), an 86.4% relative decline. Promptwatch calls this provisional. On 8 Aug, "site:" fan-out queries jumped from 0.37% to 16.8% of all fan-outs — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/geo/reddit-chatgpt-citation-drop-unexplained/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/why-reddits-chatgpt-citation-drop-isnt-fully-explained/534572/). Reports conflict on whether OpenAI commented. One summary says OpenAI told Yahoo Tech it doesn't set fixed visibility per site and still cites Reddit; others say no OpenAI statement exists — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/editorial/reddits-chatgpt-citations-collapsed-overnight/).
- **Conflict:** Ahrefs' September 2026 data still has Reddit at 16.8% of ChatGPT citations — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/) — which is irreconcilable with Promptwatch's 0.52%. They are likely measuring different things (prompt sets or collection methods).
- **Reddit for agency queries:** 1% of ChatGPT citations in Onely's 31-question agency test (Oct 2026) — [Onely](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/).
- **YouTube:** YouTube mentions (title, transcript, description) correlated about 0.737 with AI visibility across ChatGPT, AI Mode and AIO in Ahrefs' 75k-brand study (Dec 2025) — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ahrefs-youtube-mentions-ai-visibility-brand-search). YouTube was 2.5% of ChatGPT citations in Sept 2026 — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/) — and #1 for B2B queries across engines — [Social Media Today/Meltwater](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/report-looks-at-how-linkedin-is-dominating-b2b-queries-in-ai-chatbots/820946/).
- **LinkedIn:**
  - Rose from #11 to #5 in ChatGPT, Nov 2025–Feb 2026 — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/linkedin-is-the-most-cited-domain-for-professional-queries-in-ai-search).
  - 59% of LinkedIn citations in ChatGPT Search and AI Mode come from individuals, not company pages — [The State of Brand](https://www.thestateofbrand.com/news/linkedin-top-ai-citation-for-b2b).
  - Semrush (325k prompts, Jan–Feb 2026) found ChatGPT citing LinkedIn in 14.3% of responses — [Omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/linkedin-b2b-ai-citations-data).
  - LinkedIn's share of social citations in AI search rose from 7.8% (Jan) to 11.7% (May) — [Otterly](https://otterly.ai/blog/linkedin-ai-search-citations-study/).
- **Reddit seeding case (self-reported):** Nicely Network claims that Reddit discussions drove 210x more ChatGPT mentions for a furniture client. It also claims ten Reddit discussions lifted an antivirus brand's ChatGPT share of voice from 6% to 17% — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/best-ai-seo-agency-for-brand-mentions-in-chatgpt-2026-nicely-network-recognised-by-better-business-advice-302909944.html). This is an unverified award press release.

### Inferences
- For agencies, Reddit is a "nice to have" with high platform risk. Its ChatGPT citation share can swing by an order of magnitude in days, and in the one agency-query test it was negligible. Genuine participation in r/marketing, r/SEO, r/smallbusiness and city subreddits may still feed training data and Google, but it should not be the core lever.
- Founder or individual LinkedIn content and YouTube appearances (own channel plus guesting on others' channels and podcasts that publish on YouTube with transcripts) look like the most durable "community" signals for B2B agencies in 2026.
- Astroturfed Reddit seeding carries ban risk and may breach FTC endorsement and fake-review rules. That inference rests on Peev's legal note about hidden ownership.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 data on Quora's citation share in ChatGPT.
- No podcast-specific study was found. The YouTube transcript correlation is the closest proxy.
- The cause of either Reddit drop has not been confirmed by OpenAI.

## Brand mentions vs backlinks (Ahrefs 75k study and others)

### Takeaway
Correlation studies consistently find that unlinked brand mentions, especially on YouTube, track AI visibility far better than backlinks or Domain Rating. In ChatGPT specifically, classic authority metrics have the weakest correlation of the three platforms Ahrefs measured. All of this evidence is correlational, and the sample skews to established sites.

### Cited Findings
- **Original Ahrefs study (May 2025, AI Overviews only, flagged older):** Spearman correlations were branded web mentions 0.664, branded anchors 0.527, branded search volume 0.392, DR 0.326 and backlinks 0.218 — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/branded-web-mentions-visibility-ai-search/).
- **Expanded study (Dec 2025; ChatGPT, AI Mode, AIO):**
  - YouTube mentions were the top correlate at about 0.737, with YouTube mention impressions about 0.717.
  - Branded web mentions ranged 0.656–0.709.
  - ChatGPT's figures were branded search volume 0.352 and DR 0.266, the weakest authority correlations of the three platforms. AI Mode had the strongest (branded anchors 0.628) and was described as a "consensus engine".
  - Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ahrefs-youtube-mentions-ai-visibility-brand-search); [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations).
  - One secondary source gives per-engine branded web mention correlations of AI Mode 0.709, ChatGPT 0.664 and AIO 0.656. That conflicts with sources citing 0.664 as the original AIO figure — [Blck Alpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/seo-geo/geo-generative-engine-optimization/brand-mentions-vs-backlinks-the-ahrefs-75k-brand-study).
- **Caveats on the 75k study:** the sample was built from domains with DR > 40 (established brands), it uses Ahrefs' own Brand Radar data, and Ahrefs itself states that correlation is not causation — [Medium summary](https://medium.com/@lorenzoswanson786/ahrefs-studied-75-000-brands-brand-mentions-beat-backlinks-3-to-1-for-ai-visibility-e86ef9f6baba); [Dofollow Digest](https://dofollow-digest.beehiiv.com/p/dofollow-digest-55-what-actually-drives-ai-visibility-75k-brands-studied).
- **Retrieval relevance:** an Ahrefs study of 1.4M ChatGPT prompts concluded that relevance does the heavy lifting and freshness is mainly a tie-breaker, especially for news — [The Visibility Report](https://thevisibilityreport.com/posts/visibility-13-ahrefs-studied-1-4m-chatgpt-prompts-here-s-why-some-pages-get-cited-and-others-don-t). ChatGPT appears to prioritize original sources over summarizers — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-most-cited-sites-primary-sources-ahrefs/592682/).

### Inferences
- Mentions likely matter for two reasons: they are what ends up in pre-training data, and they are what fan-out retrieval finds. Mentions inside "best X" and comparison contexts are probably worth more than generic mentions.
- Because the 75k sample excludes small sites (DR < 40), the effect sizes may not transfer to small local agencies. For them, a handful of topical third-party mentions (local press, chamber of commerce, client case studies on client sites, partner directories) is the realistic equivalent.

### Gaps
- No study controls for brand size to show mentions causing ChatGPT recommendations.
- No mention-vs-backlink study is specific to service businesses.

## Entity signals: Wikipedia, Wikidata, Crunchbase, NAP consistency, knowledge graph

### Takeaway
Wikipedia is one of ChatGPT's top two cited domains (7–13% of citations), but most small agencies cannot meet Wikipedia's notability bar. Recommendations to build a Wikidata entry, a Crunchbase profile and consistent NAP data are widespread in practitioner content, but I found no measured evidence that they cause ChatGPT recommendations. Treat them as low-cost hygiene, not a lever.

### Cited Findings
- **Wikipedia's share:** 7.0% of ChatGPT citations in Sept 2026 (8.9% in July) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/). Similarweb puts it at 13.15% (US, Jan–Feb 2026) — [5W release](https://martechedge.com/press-release/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds----wsj-nyt-and-bloomberg-do-not-appear-in-the-top-20).
- **Notability bar:** Wikipedia's guideline for companies requires significant coverage in multiple independent, reliable secondary sources — [Wikipedia: Notability (organizations and companies)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(organizations_and_companies)).
- **Wikidata:** practitioners promote Wikidata as a lower-barrier alternative ("about 2 hours to build"), but no source cites a measured before/after result — [ARGEO](https://www.argeo.ai/en/blog/knowledge-graph-wikidata-optimization); [Wikibusines](https://www.wikibusines.net/wikidata-seo-knowledge-graph). Wikidata can link to Crunchbase via an organization ID property, and guides recommend schema.org sameAs links — [ARGEO](https://www.argeo.ai/en/blog/knowledge-graph-wikidata-optimization).
- **Knowledge graph:** ChatGPT recognizes brands that are in Google's Knowledge Graph but absent from Wikidata, so Wikidata is not the sole entity source. Claims that OpenAI is building its own graph are speculative — [LLMrefs](https://llmrefs.com/blog/chatgpt-entities).
- **Vendor opinion:** an agency argues that lacking a Wikipedia page is why ChatGPT won't recommend a brand. This is opinion without data — [SF Digital](https://www.sfdigital.co.uk/blog/wikipedia-page-chatgpt-brand-recommendations/).
- **Entity clarity:** practitioner explanations say ChatGPT won't cite a site it can't confidently identify (ambiguous entity, blocked crawlers, missing schema) — [Elite AI Advantage](https://eliteaiadvantage.com/white-papers/why-chatgpt-wont-cite-your-business); [Growtika](https://growtika.com/geo-hub/chatgpt-visits-cites-competitors).
- **Local:** answers were largely grounded in Google Business Profile fields (name, phone, website, rating, review count) in local tests — [LinkTech](https://linktechsolutions.io/insights/how-chatgpt-picks-local-businesses); [Elev8](https://www.elev8operations.com/guides/ai-search-statistics-for-local-businesses-2026).

### Inferences
- For a small agency, the practical entity stack is: consistent name, address, phone and description across GBP, Clutch, LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase and Bing Places; Organization/LocalBusiness schema with sameAs links; and possibly a Wikidata item if it can be referenced to independent sources (Wikidata also has notability rules). This mainly reduces entity ambiguity, for example when an agency name collides with another business. It is not shown to drive recommendations on its own.
- Effort spent trying to force a Wikipedia article is likely wasted, and such an article risks deletion. Earned press coverage is the precondition for notability anyway, so the PR lever comes first.

### Gaps
- No controlled study of Wikidata, Crunchbase or NAP consistency effects on ChatGPT mentions.
- No evidence was found on Bing Places specifically, despite ChatGPT's historical Bing grounding.

## Digital PR, original research, awards and earned media

### Takeaway
Earned media makes up the large majority of what AI assistants cite: 84% of 25M+ links in Muck Rack's data. Syndicated earned stories showed large citation lifts in Stacker's (small, self-interested) study. Original data and primary sources appear to be favoured by ChatGPT. Paid "awards" have no evidence base.

### Cited Findings
- **Muck Rack (May 2026):** 25M+ links cited by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini across 17 industries. 84% were earned media, journalism was 27%, and paid/advertorial content was 0.3%. ChatGPT cites sources in 96% of responses, averaging 5 citations, versus Claude at 55% and Gemini at 82% — [Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com/blog/what-is-ai-reading-may-2026). Earlier editions had earned media at 82–89% — [TechIntelPro](https://techintelpro.com/news/muck-rack-may-2026-study-earned-media-drives-84-of-ai-citations). Muck Rack sells PR software.
- **Stacker + Scrunch (March 2026):** 87 stories, 30 clients, 2,600+ prompts across 8 AI platforms. Syndicated stories gained a median 239% more AI citations than brand-owned content alone, and cross-platform coverage rose from 5.4% to 17.9%. A Dec 2025 version reported a 325% lift (8% citation rate on the brand site vs 34% when syndicated) — [Stacker](https://stacker.com/blog/how-to-get-your-brand-content-cited-by-ai-what-the-data-shows). Stacker sells syndication, and one critique notes the study does not establish recommendation lift or revenue effect — [AuthorityTech](https://authoritytech.io/blog/machine-relations-evidence-earned-media-ai-citations).
- **Original sources:** ChatGPT appears to prioritize original sources over sites that summarize them — [Search Engine Journal on Ahrefs](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-most-cited-sites-primary-sources-ahrefs/592682/).
- **Awards:** "0 studies" measure whether paid awards raise AI visibility, and every claim found came from sellers of awards or PR — [Stanislav Peev](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/owned-review-sites-ai-visibility/). Example of award-as-PR: Nicely Network "recognised by Better Business Advice" as best AI SEO agency for ChatGPT mentions — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/best-ai-seo-agency-for-brand-mentions-in-chatgpt-2026-nicely-network-recognised-by-better-business-advice-302909944.html).
- **Publisher concentration:** Forbes is a top-5 ChatGPT domain (3.1% in Sept 2026) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/). WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg and FT are not in the top 20 — [5W release](https://martechedge.com/press-release/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds----wsj-nyt-and-bloomberg-do-not-appear-in-the-top-20).

### Inferences
- For agencies, the highest-yield PR is publishing original data, such as an annual "state of [industry/city] websites/SEO" study, that trade and local media cite. It creates earned mentions and makes the agency a primary source that ChatGPT prefers to cite.
- Contributor content on Forbes-type sites (e.g., Forbes Agency Council) is often pay-to-join. It is visible to ChatGPT, but the value of paid-membership bylines vs editorial coverage is unmeasured.

### Gaps
- No study measures whether PR-driven citations translate into the agency being recommended, as opposed to just cited, for "best agency" prompts.

## Documented case studies (with numbers) of agencies/B2B service firms increasing ChatGPT mentions via off-site work

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous, independently verified case study of an agency increasing ChatGPT recommendations through off-site work. The best-documented controlled example is Ahrefs' own self-promotion experiment, which concerns a product and a conference, not an agency. Other "case studies" are self-reported marketing claims.

### Cited Findings
- **Ahrefs Evolve (new brand), Feb–May 2026:** entered 72 previously empty answer slots across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot; 82% of the new mentions came in answers citing Ahrefs' own pages; 66.4% inclusion on the narrow query vs 15.8% on the broad one — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/self-promotional-content-ai-seo-experiment/).
- **Nicely Network (self-reported):**
  - Furniture client: $150,000 tracked sales, 21,190 clicks, 1M discussion views, 210x ChatGPT mentions and 35x AI Overview mentions from Reddit discussions.
  - Cybersecurity client: 10 Reddit discussions produced 250+ citations, and ChatGPT share of voice went from 6% to 17%.
  - Source: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/best-ai-seo-agency-for-brand-mentions-in-chatgpt-2026-nicely-network-recognised-by-better-business-advice-302909944.html). Unverified, and published in an award press release.
- **Practitioner test of a real agency:** the agency was absent from every listicle feeding ChatGPT's "best agencies" answers, which explains its absence from the answers. No before/after numbers — [Todd O'Rourke](https://toddmorourke.com/learn/how-chatgpt-picks-sources/).
- **Onely:** in its research on "best ChatGPT SEO agencies", no AI engine showed evidence that any recommended agency had ever got a client named in ChatGPT — [Onely](https://www.onely.com/blog/best-chatgpt-seo-agencies/).
- **Go Fish Digital:** markets ChatGPT optimization, but its case-study page contains no ChatGPT-specific results — [Go Fish Digital case studies](https://gofishdigital.com/case-studies).
- **Anecdote:** an agency claims a client appeared in ChatGPT within five weeks of adding schema markup. This is single and unverified (reported in [Wikidata/knowledge-graph roundup](https://www.argeo.ai/en/blog/knowledge-graph-wikidata-optimization) search results).

### Inferences
- The lack of verified agency case studies is itself a finding. Claims of ChatGPT-mention lifts should be judged on baseline, prompt set, number of runs, and whether the claim is "mentioned" or "recommended".

### Gaps
- No case study with a disclosed prompt set, baseline and follow-up for an agency or B2B service firm was found.

## Canada-specific nuances

### Takeaway
I found no Canada-specific study of ChatGPT citation sources. Most datasets (Similarweb's ~600k events, BrightLocal's survey, Ahrefs' data, and the search tool used here) are US-centric. Canadian "best agency" listicles exist on directories and agency blogs, many of them self-promotional.

### Cited Findings
- **Canada-specific lists exist on these sites:**
  - DesignRush ("Top Digital Agencies in Canada") — [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/digital-agencies/ca)
  - Digital Agency Network ("Top AI Marketing Agencies in Canada 2026") — [DAN](https://digitalagencynetwork.com/agencies/canada/ai-marketing/)
  - Lengreo (Canada and Quebec "ChatGPT marketing agencies" lists) — [Lengreo Canada](https://lengreo.com/chatgpt-marketing-agencies-canada/); [Lengreo Quebec](https://lengreo.com/chatgpt-marketing-agencies-quebec/)
  - Haligonia (Halifax local outlet top 5) — [Haligonia](https://haligonia.ca/top-5-digital-marketing-agencies-in-canada-316753/)
  - Agency blogs such as Infinity Digital (ranks itself #1) and 88gravity — [Infinity Digital](https://infinitydigital.ca/blog/10-best-chatgpt-marketing-companies-in-canada/); [88gravity](https://www.88gravity.com/blog/top-digital-marketing-companies-in-canada-you-can-trust-in-2026)
- **US-only data:** the Similarweb figures are US-only — [5W release](https://martechedge.com/press-release/wikipedia-and-reddit-now-drive-over-25-of-chatgpt-citations-in-the-us-new-5w-research-finds----wsj-nyt-and-bloomberg-do-not-appear-in-the-top-20) — and BrightLocal's survey covered US adults — [BCC Interactive](https://www.bccinteractive.com/blog/local-ai-search-statistics/).

### Inferences
- For a Canadian agency, the tactical translation is to get onto city- and province-qualified lists (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Halifax; plus French-language lists for Quebec) that ChatGPT retrieves for prompts like "best web design agency in Halifax". Local Canadian media and business publications (e.g., city outlets like Haligonia) are plausible earned-mention targets. Bilingual listings may matter for Quebec prompts. All of this is inferred, not measured.

### Gaps
- No data on whether ChatGPT localizes citations for Canadian users (e.g., more .ca sources), on Canadian directories (e.g., Yellow Pages Canada, Canadian chamber directories) in AI citations, or on Bing Places/Yelp Canada.
- The search tool used was US-only, which limited discovery of Canadian sources.
